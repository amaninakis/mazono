# Mazono name and logo

The Mazono code is free software under the [GNU AGPL-3.0](LICENSE). The **name "Mazono"** and the
**Mazono logo, icon and wordmark** (the files in [`brand/`](brand) and the icons in `app/public/`)
are not covered by that licence. They belong to Andreas Maninakis and identify the official Mazono
app and service.

## You may, without asking

- Say that your work is "based on Mazono", "a fork of Mazono" or "compatible with Mazono".
- Run your own unmodified copy of Mazono for yourself, your family, your farm or your cooperative,
  with the name and logo as they are.
- Write about Mazono, and use the logo in articles, talks or reviews about it.

## Please ask first

- Before offering Mazono, or a changed version of it, as a service to others under the Mazono name.
- Before using the name or logo on products, merchandise, or anything sold.

## Not allowed

- Publishing a changed version (a fork) under the name Mazono, or with the Mazono logo or icon. A fork
  must have its own name and logo, different enough that nobody mistakes it for Mazono.
- Using a name or logo that is confusingly similar (for example "Mazono Pro" or a recoloured olive
  icon) for a fork, app or service.
- Suggesting that Mazono or its maintainer endorses, supports or is responsible for your version.

If you fork Mazono, change the name, the icons and the wordmark (in `brand/`, `app/public/`,
`app/index.html` and the `brand` texts in the translation files) before you publish it.

Questions: open an issue.
