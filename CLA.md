Version 1.0, November 2026

permission to use it, including under licences other than the AGPL. This lets Mazono stay open source
while it can also offer paid plans, paid features or a commercial licence, and publish the app in app
stores.

You accept this agreement by commenting on your pull request with the sentence the CLA bot asks for.
It covers that contribution and every later contribution you make to Mazono.
